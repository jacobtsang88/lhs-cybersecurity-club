Our Mission & Team

The Cybersecurity Club bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world scenarios, equips members with practical skills, teaches ethical hacking techniques, and provides knowledge of emerging threats. Through hands-on exercises and workshops, we seek to foster innovation and critical thinking in the field of cybersecurity.
